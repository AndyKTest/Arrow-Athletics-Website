What is the Arrow League?

Summer soccer that actually counts.

The Arrow League brings together high school boys and girls across Massachusetts, giving them a chance to keep playing through the summer offseason. With a structured format, clear guidelines, a divisional setup and referees, the league turns casual summer pickup into a consistent opportunity to sharpen your skills, build team chemistry, and get reps with others who love the game.

Boys' and girls' teams each play in Division 1, 2 or 3, and our league coordinators work with every team to find the right fit.

How it works

Built like a real season

Two Games a Week

Five weeks of pool play from July into August, on weeknights between 4 and 9 p.m.

A Referee Every Game

We handle scheduling, referees and standings, so teams can focus on playing.

The Arrow Cup

Top teams from each pool meet in a knockout bracket on the final weekend. Six divisions, six champions.
