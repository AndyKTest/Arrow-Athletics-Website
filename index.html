What is Arrow Cup?

A new summer tradition, built one season at a time.

Arrow Cup started with a simple idea: give girls' and boys' soccer players a competitive, well-run summer league where the games matter, the coaching is real, and every team gets a shot at the title. This year it's soccer. Down the road, we're planning to bring in more sports — lacrosse and beyond.

Whether your player is lacing up for their first season or chasing a championship, Arrow Cup is built to be the highlight of their summer.
