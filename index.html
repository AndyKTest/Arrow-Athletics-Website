What is the Arrow Cup?

Summer soccer that actually counts.

Most high school players spend the summer scrimmaging in pickup games or waiting for preseason. Arrow Cup gives them somewhere real to play — a structured league with standings, officials, and a title on the line, running through the heart of the summer.

Teams enter as school squads or as combined rosters of high school players. Boys' and girls' divisions run side by side, each split into varsity and JV so everyone competes at the right level.
