About Us

Built for the love of the game.

Arrow Cup is a summer soccer league for girls and boys — and the beginning of something bigger.

Our Story

Why we started Arrow Cup

Arrow Athletics started with a straightforward goal: build a summer league that takes youth soccer seriously without losing the fun of it. That means real schedules, real officiating, and a championship weekend worth training for — for girls' and boys' teams alike.

This first season, we're focused entirely on soccer. As Arrow Cup grows, the plan is to expand into more sports — lacrosse is next on the list — so the league can grow with the athletes in it.

What We're About

The values behind the league

Community

Families, coaches, and players building something together every summer.

Real Competition

A genuine season and playoff bracket — not just a weekend jamboree.

Character

Sportsmanship and effort matter here as much as the final score.

Growth

Soccer first — with more sports on the way as the league grows.
