About Us

Who we are

Arrow Athletics is a newly formed organization built to give high school boys and girls a unique opportunity to compete over the summer.

Our Story

Why we started the Arrow League

My brother Greg and I started Arrow Athletics because, like a lot of the players who'll fill these divisions, we loved playing for our high school team — and we missed it the second the fall season ended. We both went on to play in college, and we watched plenty of teammates go the club route too. That's real competition, and we don't take anything away from it. But none of it replaces playing for your school, with your own classmates on the sideline cheering you on.

So we built the league we wished had existed: a summer season where high school players keep playing as a team, with real divisions, real referees, and standings that actually matter — not a pickup run to kill time before preseason.

Our first season, Summer 2027, brings together high school teams from across Massachusetts. Teams play the season in pools, then meet on the final weekend for the Arrow Cup, where the top teams from each pool play for the title. Over time we want to grow it across New England, bringing together high school teams that have never had a reason, or a chance, to play each other.

— Andrew Kovacs, Co-Founder

Our Mission

A high-quality summer league that builds stronger players, better teams, and a greater sense of connection to all competition.

Why the Arrow League

Why choose the Arrow League

Your school, your summer

Keep your team together and keep representing your school, with your own players and your own coaches.

Play at your level

We work with each team to find the right division, so every team finds games that fit.

Stay sharp for fall

Ten games over five weeks keep your players in rhythm heading into the fall season.

College coach exposure

Put your players in front of college coaches and into the recruiting conversation.

Best team in the state

Compete against teams from across Massachusetts to find out who the best team in the state really is.

We run the league

Scheduling, referees and standings are handled by us, so you can focus on coaching, playing and developing.

And the Arrow Cup to play for, with a champion crowned in every division.
