Registration Is Open for Summer 2026

June 1, 2026

Registration for the Summer 2026 Arrow Cup is open. Four divisions are running this summer — boys' varsity, boys' JV, girls' varsity and girls' JV — all for athletes in grades 9–12.

What a team gets

Every registered team plays a full five-week regular season — two games a week, ten games total — with officiated matches, published standings, and a place in the championship bracket in August.

  • Two 11v11 fixtures a week through June and July
  • Certified referees on every match
  • Standings tracked and published through the season
  • A guaranteed spot in championship weekend

Who can enter

Teams can enter as a school squad or as a combined roster of high school players from the same area. Every player must be enrolled in grades 9–12 for the upcoming school year. Rosters can carry up to 22 players.

How to register

Fill out the registration form with your team name, division, and a contact for the coach or manager. We'll reply by email with the season schedule, field locations, and the current fee schedule before anything is confirmed.

Divisions are capped, so teams that register early get first choice of match slots.

Ready to enter a team?

Registration for Summer 2026 is open for all four divisions.
