Registration Is Open for the 2026 Season

June 1, 2026

Registration for the 2026 Arrow Cup season is officially open, with divisions available for both girls' and boys' teams across all age groups.

What's included this season

Every registered team gets a full 10-week regular season, officiated matches, tracked standings, and a guaranteed spot in the end-of-summer championship bracket.

  • Girls' and boys' divisions from U8 through U18
  • Weekly league games across the summer
  • A championship weekend to close out the season

How to register

Head over to the Contact page and let us know your player's or team's age group. We'll follow up with next steps, key dates, and everything you need before the season kicks off.

Spots are limited by division, so teams that register early get first pick of game-day scheduling.
