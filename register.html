Questions

Common questions

Is this the official registration?

Not yet. This form tells us you're interested. Official registration for Summer 2027 opens soon, and we'll reach out to everyone who filled this in first.

Who can play?

High school boys and girls in Massachusetts. Teams can keep their school squad together over the summer or build a roster of high school players from the same area, up to 30 players.

How do divisions work?

Boys and girls each have Divisions 1, 2 and 3: top-level competition, competitive play for rising teams, and development. Our league coordinators work with every team to find the right fit, and each division is split into pools.

When is the season?

The first season is Summer 2027: five weeks of pool play from July into August, two weekday games a week between 4 and 9 p.m., then the Arrow Cup knockout bracket on the final weekend. Specific fields and dates will be announced soon.

What does it cost?

Team pricing is between $1,700–$1,850. Every game has a referee, and all play is fully insured.

Who coaches the team?

Each team has an adult coach who is the primary contact. Because of high school soccer rules, coaches can be parents, graduated former players, and more. Each team also elects at least two captains.

Can we talk it through first?

Yes. We're happy to set up a call or in-person meeting. Email info@arrow-athletics.com or call +1 (774) 270-9555.

The Arrow League is a summer soccer league for high school boys' and girls' teams across Massachusetts — six divisions, real referees, and the Arrow Cup to finish the summer.
