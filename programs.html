Boys' & Girls' Divisions

Six divisions across Massachusetts: Divisions 1, 2 and 3 for boys and for girls. Five weeks of pool play, then the Arrow Cup.

What is the Arrow League?

The Arrow League brings together high school boys and girls across Massachusetts, giving them a chance to keep playing through the summer offseason. With a structured format, clear guidelines, a divisional setup and referees, the league turns casual summer pickup into a consistent opportunity to sharpen your skills, build team chemistry, and get reps with others who love the game.

Season Format

How the summer runs

1

Get placed in a division

  • Work with our coordinators to find the right fit
  • Boys and girls each have Divisions 1, 2 and 3
  • Each division is split into pools
2

Pool play

  • Five weeks, July into August
  • Two weekday games a week, 4 to 9 p.m.
  • Pool results decide who advances to the Arrow Cup
3

The Arrow Cup

  • A knockout bracket on the final weekend of the season
  • Top teams from each pool face off over three days
  • Every game is win or go home
4

Six champions

  • One title for each division
  • Boys and girls Divisions 1, 2 and 3
  • Medals, a trophy, and the honor of winning the Arrow Cup

Six divisions. Six champions.

Coaches and captains

  • Each team has an adult coach who is the primary contact and helps keep team structure during league play. Because of high school soccer rules, coaches can be parents, graduated former players, and more.
  • Each team elects at least two captains to lead the team, on the field and off it.
  • Arrow Athletics works with the coach and captains all season to keep fields and players available.

Fields and times

  • Two weekday games a week, between 4 and 9 p.m.
  • Referees and scheduling handled by Arrow Athletics
  • Game times set with coaches and referees around field availability
  • Specific fields and dates announced soon

Dates and fees

  • First season in Summer 2027, across Massachusetts
  • Team pricing between $1,700–$1,850
  • Up to 30 players per team
  • Strict league rules and full insurance for all play
